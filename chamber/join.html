NP Membership

Designed for registered non-profit organizations serving the Maipú community. There is no membership fee.

  • Listing in the online business directory
  • Access to Chamber networking events
  • Invitations to community volunteer fairs

Bronze Membership

A great starting point for small local businesses looking to grow their presence in Maipú.

  • Everything in NP Membership
  • Discounted tickets to Chamber events
  • Basic directory listing with contact info

Silver Membership

For growing businesses that want more exposure and training opportunities.

  • Everything in Bronze Membership
  • Quarterly business training workshops
  • Featured placement in the directory listing

Gold Membership

Our top membership tier, built for businesses that want maximum visibility and support.

  • Everything in Silver Membership
  • Home page spotlight rotation
  • Free admission for staff to all Chamber events
